## Final Project Ideas

### International Student Enrollment in the U.S.

I want to explore how international student enrollment in the U.S. has changed over time. I am also interested in how enrollment from Nepal compares with enrollment from other countries.

I plan to use the Open Doors datasets to make a line chart of overall enrollment over time and a chart comparing selected countries in years with available data. As an international student from Nepal, I am curious to see how the trends for Nepal compare with the overall trend.
